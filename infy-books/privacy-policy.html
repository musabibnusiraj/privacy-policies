1. Introduction

InfyBooks ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of users of AddonInvoice, our web-based invoice and business management platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

By using AddonInvoice, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Account & Profile Information

  • Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Company name, business address, phone number, and logo (provided during setup).

b) Business Data

  • Customer and vendor contact details you enter into the platform.
  • Invoices, estimates, purchase orders, expenses, and payments you create.
  • Recurring invoice schedules and payment records.

c) Payment Information

  • Billing name, address, and payment card details for subscription payments. Payment card data is processed securely by our payment gateway provider and is never stored on our servers.

d) Usage & Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type, device information, and operating system.
  • Pages visited, features used, and session duration (collected via server logs and analytics).
  • Error reports and diagnostic data to help us improve the platform.

e) Communications

  • Messages sent to our support team and any feedback you provide.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve AddonInvoice and its features.
  • Process subscription payments and send billing receipts.
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • Send transactional emails (invoices, estimates, payment notifications).
  • Respond to support requests and customer inquiries.
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience and fix issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We may share data only with:

  • Payment processors to handle subscription billing securely (e.g., Stripe, PayHere).
  • Cloud hosting providers for platform infrastructure (e.g., servers, file storage).
  • Email service providers to deliver transactional and notification emails.
  • Analytics tools to monitor platform performance and usage (anonymized where possible).
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers with appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access controls limiting who can view your data.
  • Regular backups to prevent data loss.
  • Monitoring and intrusion detection systems.

While we implement industry-standard security practices, no system is 100% immune to breaches. We will notify you promptly if a breach affecting your data occurs.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active. If you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your data within 90 days, unless retention is required by law (e.g., for tax or legal compliance purposes).

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in between sessions.
  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Collect anonymous analytics data to improve the platform.

You can manage cookie settings in your browser. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the platform.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your personal data (right to be forgotten).
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at musab.dot@gmail.com.

AddonInvoice is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

If you access our services from outside the country where our servers are located, your data may be transferred internationally. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.
