We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for new subscribers. If you are not satisfied with AddonInvoice within the first 30 days of your initial paid subscription, you may request a full refund. This guarantee applies once per customer account and is not applicable to renewals or upgrades.

Refunds may be granted under the following conditions:

  • The refund request is submitted within 30 days of the initial payment.

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Requests submitted more than 30 days after the billing date.

To request a refund, please contact us at musab.dot@gmail.com with:
