Privacy-First & Offline by Default

Mini Billy is built as an offline-first Point of Sale app. All business data — inventory, sales transactions, customer lists, prices, debt records, and receipts — is stored locally on your physical device. No account is needed to use Mini Billy, including on the Free plan. Sign-in is optional on the Free plan and required for Pro and above, and uses Google Sign-In. If you do not sign in, nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share or back it up. Accounts and cloud features exist only in the optional paid plans (Section 7A) and are used only if you sign in and turn them on. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or rent your business records to anyone.

1. Overview & Purpose 🔗

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mini Billy mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the “App”), developed and published by AIMZCODE (“we”, “us”, or “our”), available for Android via the Google Play Store and iOS via the Apple App Store.

The App is designed to help small shop owners, merchants, retail stores, and service providers process sales, manage product inventory, track customer credit/debts, print receipts via Bluetooth thermal printers, and generate sales reports completely offline without requiring accounts, logins, or remote server storage. Optional paid plans add sign-in, Google Drive backup, and cloud backup and sync; those are described in Section 7A.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable global privacy regulations.

2. Information We Do Not Collect 🔗

Mini Billy operates locally on your smartphone or tablet. If you do not sign in and do not turn on cloud features (on any plan, including Free), AIMZCODE does not collect, receive, store, or monitor any of the following. In every case we never collect:

  • No Account Needed, Even on the Free Plan: No registration, email address, password, or profile is needed to use the App. Your Google account details are collected only if you choose to sign in, or must sign in for a Pro or higher plan (Section 7A).
  • No Analytics or Tracking IDs: We do not integrate third-party mobile measurement partners (MMPs), advertising trackers, or tracking SDKs (such as Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer, etc.).
  • No Location Data: The App does not track, collect, or store your precise (GPS) or approximate geographic location.
  • No Payment Card Numbers: We never see or store your card details for plan purchases; those are handled by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or your bank (Section 7A). While the App lets you record that a customer paid via card, bank transfer, or QR Pay for bookkeeping purposes, the App does not process card numbers, CVVs, PINs, or financial transactions. Cash and card processing remains entirely external to the App.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not read or transmit the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

3. Data Processed & Stored Locally on Your Device 🔗

Any data you enter into Mini Billy remains inside the App’s sandboxed local storage on your device. This data includes:

  • Product Catalog: Item names, categories, variants (size, colour), prices, cost prices, SKUs, barcodes, and units of measurement.
  • Product & Shop Images: Product photos, shop logo, and merchant payment QR codes selected by you from your device’s camera or photo library.
  • Sales & Transaction History: Order numbers, timestamps, purchased products, quantities, discounts, tax rates, payment methods chosen (e.g., Cash, Card, Credit, QR Pay), and refunds or corrections.
  • Customer Records: Optional customer names, phone numbers, customer classification tiers (e.g., wholesaler, retailer, consumer), customer balances, and credit/debt transaction logs that you manually input.
  • Daily Cash Closing Reports: Shift and daily cash counts, expected cash totals, and closing notes.
  • Store Configuration: Shop name, shop address, contact phone number, receipt header/footer messages, and preferred currency (e.g., Rs, $, €, etc.) and language settings (English, Tamil, Sinhala).

This information is stored in a private local file (mini_pos.json) managed exclusively by your device’s operating system.

7. Third-Party Services & Network Activity 🔗

Mini Billy is designed to work offline. Without signing in, the App makes network requests only on your direct action. If you sign in, it also contacts our servers to sign you in, check your plan and, if enabled, back up and sync, as described in Section 7A. The user-initiated interactions are:

  • Optional Google Sheets Catalog Import: If you choose to import product inventory from a public or shared Google Sheets document, the App sends a single direct HTTP request to download that public spreadsheet data into your local device catalog. No merchant credentials or sales records are transmitted during this operation.
  • Native OS Share Sheet: If you choose to export reports (CSV/PDF) or backup files using WhatsApp, Gmail, Google Drive, or AirDrop, the transmission is handled directly by your device’s operating system and chosen third-party application under their respective privacy policies.

7A. Optional Accounts, Cloud & Paid Plans 🔗

Mini Billy offers four plans. You sign in with Google Sign-In. Signing in is optional on Free and required on Pro, Pro Max and Ultra Pro Max. Backing up to Google Drive needs sign-in on every plan. Free and Pro work offline; Pro Max and Ultra Pro Max add cloud backup and sync. Features that need an account or internet stay off until you sign in and turn them on.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Google account details shared by Google Sign-In: your email address, name and Google user ID. We use them to sign you in and link your plan to you. We never receive your Google password.
  • Plan and purchase status (plan, start and end date, and a purchase reference), to unlock the features you paid for. We do not receive your card number.
  • Staff users (Pro Max and Ultra Pro Max): the names or emails and roles of the people you add to your account, as you enter them.
  • Shop data you sync, only when you turn on cloud backup and sync: your products, sales, customers and debts, stock records, settings, and photos. This is business data you entered; we use it only to back up, restore and sync it for you and your users. We do not read it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

How plans are paid for

  • Google Play or Apple App Store billing: payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their terms and privacy policies. We receive a purchase confirmation and reference, not your card or bank details.
  • Bank transfer: you pay AIMZCODE by bank transfer and send us the payment slip or reference (for example by email or WhatsApp). We use it only to confirm the payment and activate your plan, and keep payment records as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Where cloud data is stored and who processes it

Sign-in, plan status and (for Pro Max and Ultra Pro Max) synced shop data are stored on our own server. We run the server software ourselves (the open-source Supabase platform) on a server we rent from the hosting company Contabo GmbH (Germany), which acts as our data processor and handles the data only to keep the server running. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access is limited so that each account can read only its own data. Backups you send to your own Google Drive are stored by Google in your Drive; the App asks Google for access only to the backup files it creates, and you can remove that access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Offline use

On Pro, Pro Max and Ultra Pro Max the App always works from a database on your device, so a lost connection never stops you from selling. While offline it keeps working on that local data for a limited time. When the internet returns, the App automatically checks your plan again and, on Pro Max and Ultra Pro Max, syncs the changes made offline to the cloud. On Pro, the re-check confirms your licence and sends any pending Google Drive backup.

Your choices

  • You can use the Free plan without ever signing in. Signing in on Free links your Google account to the App and lets you back up to your Google Drive; it does not upload your shop data to our servers unless you are on a plan with cloud sync and turn it on.
  • You can turn off cloud sync, sign out, or export all your data as a JSON backup and CSV files at any time.
  • If a paid plan ends or you return to Free, your data stays on your device and you can keep selling within the Free plan limits.

8. Data Retention, Backups & Complete Deletion 🔗

Data Ownership & Retention

Because all data is stored on your device, you maintain full and absolute ownership of your business data. Data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the App installed.

Local Backups

Mini Billy includes built-in safeguards to protect your business records against accidental loss:

  • Automatic Daily Rolling Backups: The App maintains local snapshots of your data on the device, keeping the last 7 daily backups automatically.
  • Manual Backup & Restore: You can create complete backup archives (including products, history, and photos) at any time and store them safely on your local storage, external SD card, or personal cloud storage.

Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play’s Data Deletion Policy and Apple’s User Privacy Guidelines:

  • If you never signed in, we hold no account or server data, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • If you created an account, you can delete your account in the App (More › Account › Delete account), or email us at the address in Section 12. We delete your account, plan record and all synced cloud data within 30 days, except payment records we must keep for accounting or tax law. Data on your own devices and in your own Google Drive is not affected; you can delete it yourself.
  • If a yearly plan ends and is not renewed, synced cloud data is kept for 30 days so you can renew or download it, then deleted.
  • To completely and permanently delete all data stored in Mini Billy, delete the data on your device by clearing the App Storage / Cache in your device’s Settings or uninstall the App. All local databases, catalogs, customer lists, and backup files stored within the App’s sandboxed directory will be immediately and irreversibly erased.

9. User Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Privacy) 🔗

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can export your complete product catalog, sales records, and business data at any time via the App’s CSV and JSON backup export tools.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit, correct, or update any product, customer, or business information directly within the App interface.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You can delete individual records or wipe the database by clearing app data, and you can delete your account and cloud data as described in Section 8.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data or business information under any circumstances, and we do not share data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. Children’s Privacy 🔗

Mini Billy is a point of sale and inventory management tool intended for commercial business use by adults and business owners. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information within the App on a device, such data can be permanently removed by clearing the App’s data on that device.
