Privacy-First and Fully Offline

Mini Billy works completely offline. Everything you enter — products, sales, customers, debts, stock records, photos and settings — is stored only on your device. This version has no sign-in, no accounts and no cloud features, so AIMZCODE does not receive any of your data. Data leaves your device only when you choose to share, export or back it up. We use no advertising or tracking tools, and we never sell anything about you or your shop.

1. Overview & Purpose 🔗

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mini Billy app (the “App”) for Android and iOS, made by AIMZCODE (“we”, “us”). It covers the offline edition of the App: the version without sign-in, accounts or cloud features.

The App helps shop owners sell, scan barcodes, manage stock, keep customer credit and debts, print receipts and labels on Bluetooth printers, close the day and see reports, all without an internet connection.

We follow the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (5.1), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act.

2. Information We Do Not Collect 🔗

The App runs on your phone or tablet. AIMZCODE does not collect, receive or store any of your data. In particular:

  • No account: there is no registration, email, password or profile.
  • No analytics or tracking: no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA).
  • No location: the App does not use your GPS or approximate location.
  • No payment card data: you can record that a customer paid by cash, card, bank transfer or QR code for your own books, but the App never handles card numbers, PINs or real payments.
  • No crash reports: the App does not send crash or usage reports to us.

3. Data Stored on Your Device 🔗

Everything you enter stays in the App’s private storage on your device:

  • Products: names, categories, variants, prices, costs, barcodes, units, stock, manufacture and expiry dates.
  • Images: product photos, your shop logo and payment QR code, chosen by you from the camera or photo library.
  • Sales: receipts, items, quantities, discounts, tax, payment method, refunds and corrections.
  • Customers: names, phone numbers, customer types and prices, credit and debt payments, as you enter them.
  • Stock and closing records: received stock, stock counts, restock lists and daily cash closings.
  • Settings: shop name, address and phone, receipt texts, currency, tax, language, printer and PIN lock settings. Owner and cashier PINs are stored only as salted hashes, never as the PIN itself.

  • Data used to track you: None

7. Network Activity 🔗

The App does not contact AIMZCODE servers. It uses the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Google Sheets import: if you import products from a Google Sheet link, the App downloads that sheet. Nothing from your shop is sent.
  • Sharing and links: when you share a receipt, report or backup, or open WhatsApp, email, our website or this policy, your device’s share sheet or browser handles it under that app’s own privacy policy.

The App includes Google’s Firebase core library, which starts with the App. No Firebase service that collects data (such as Analytics, Crashlytics or Cloud Messaging) is used in this edition.

8. Retention, Backups & Deletion 🔗

Your data, on your device

Your data belongs to you and stays on your device for as long as the App is installed.

Backups

  • Automatic daily backups: the App keeps the last 7 daily backup files on the device.
  • Manual backup and restore: make a full backup file at any time and keep it wherever you like.

Deleting your data

  • We hold no account or server data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • To delete everything, clear the App’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the App. Backup files you saved or shared outside the App stay where you put them.

9. Your Rights 🔗

  • Access and portability: export your products, sales and customers as CSV, or a full backup file, at any time.
  • Correction: edit any record in the App.
  • Erasure: delete records in the App, or clear its data as described in Section 8.
  • No sale of data: we do not sell, rent or share personal or business data.

10. Children’s Privacy 🔗

Mini Billy is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Policy 🔗

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A later version of the App may add optional sign-in and cloud backup; before that version is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what those features collect, and you will be able to keep using the App without them.
