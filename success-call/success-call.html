Privacy Policy for Success Call

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Thank you for choosing Success Call (“we,” “us,” “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what leaves your device. It applies to every version of Success Call: Android, iOS, Web and Windows.

In short: Success Call has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It never accesses your location. It does connect to the internet to download prayer-time data and to receive prayer notifications.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information

There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth, and we have no server that stores anything about you.

We do not access your location

Success Call does not request, access or use your device's GPS or location services on any platform.

  • Prayer times come from a zone you select manually from predefined areas in Sri Lanka (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara, Jaffna, Mullaitivu, Kilinochchi, Vavuniya, Mannar and Puttalam districts).
  • The Qibla compass uses your device's magnetic and motion sensors together with the fixed published coordinates of your selected zone. It does not read your actual position.
  • The masjid map shows a fixed map of Sri Lanka and does not center on, follow or read your device's position.

Information stored only on your device

Your settings are saved in your device's local app storage and a local database:

  • Selected prayer zone and data source
  • Iqamah offsets and prayer-time adjustments
  • Notification preferences and chosen adhan sound
  • Display preferences (theme, language, clock style, widget options)
  • A cached copy of prayer times, Hijri calendar data and the masjid map listing, so the app works offline

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Home screen widget (Android and iOS)

The optional “Next Prayer” home screen widget reads the prayer times already cached on your device to display the next prayer. It does not make its own network requests and does not send any data anywhere.

Information That Leaves Your Device

Success Call is not a fully offline app. The following things involve a network.

1. Prayer-time, calendar and masjid map data

The app downloads prayer times, Hijri calendar data and the masjid location listing from our static hosting on GitHub Pages (successcall.github.io).

These are plain file downloads — the app sends no personal data. As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) automatically processes your IP address in its standard server logs to deliver the file. We do not receive, store or have access to those logs.

2. Masjid map tiles and location data

The masjid map screen shows mosque locations across Sri Lanka. Pin locations are sourced from OpenStreetMap contributors and the Overture Maps Foundation, bundled into the static listing described above — the app does not query these projects live or send your location to them. We do not use Google Maps or any Google location data for this feature.

The map's background tiles (the map imagery itself) are loaded directly from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org) as you pan and zoom. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to OpenStreetMap's infrastructure to deliver the map image; no other data about you is sent.

3. Connectivity check

Before deciding how to deliver a prayer notification, the app makes a lightweight request to one.one.one.one (a public Cloudflare endpoint) purely to test whether an internet connection exists. No data about you is sent; Cloudflare processes your IP address as it would for any request.

4. Prayer notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Android and iOS only.

To deliver prayer-time notifications reliably, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. This involves:

  • A device registration token generated by Google, which identifies your app installation so a notification can reach it. It is not linked to your identity.
  • A topic subscription matching your selected prayer zone (for example newZones_Zone4), so your device receives alerts for the right zone. This tells our messaging provider which zone you chose, but nothing about who you are.

The token is deleted when you uninstall the app. Firebase's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising or any other tracking service.

Web

The web version stores preferences in your browser's local storage. It does not send notifications and does not use cookies or trackers.

Windows

The Windows version stores preferences locally and shows notifications through the Windows notification system. It does not use Firebase.

Sensors

The Qibla compass reads your device's magnetometer and accelerometer to work out which way the device is pointing. Sensor readings are used momentarily on your device to draw the compass and are never recorded or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Success Call is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and there are no accounts, messaging features or user-generated content.

Data Security and Retention

  • Preferences and cached prayer data are stored in your device's app-private storage, protected by your operating system.
  • We operate no user database, so there is no store of personal data to breach.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data and invalidates the notification token.

Sharing

The app can let you share a screenshot-style image of the home screen (for example, to send prayer times to someone) using your device's normal share sheet. This only happens when you tap the share button, the image is generated on your device, and we do not receive a copy of it.

Your Choices

  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your system settings at any time. This also stops zone topic subscriptions.
  • Use the app offline. Once prayer times, the Hijri calendar and the masjid map listing are cached, the app continues to work and to show alerts without a network connection (though map tile images need a connection to load).
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app, or by using Reset App in Settings.

Donations

If the app invites you to support its development, any contribution is made outside the app through the external link provided. We do not process, collect or store payment card details.
