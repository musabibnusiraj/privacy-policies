# Privacy Policy for Success Call

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing Success Call ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy
explains what information the app handles, why, and what leaves your device.
It applies to every version of Success Call: **Android, iOS, Web and Windows**.

**In short:** Success Call has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. It never accesses your location. It does connect to the internet to
download prayer-time data and to receive prayer notifications.

---

## Information We Collect

### We do not collect personal information

There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number
or date of birth, and we have no server that stores anything about you.

### We do not access your location

Success Call **does not** request, access or use your device's GPS or location
services on any platform.

- Prayer times come from a **zone you select manually** from predefined areas in
  Sri Lanka (Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara, Jaffna, Mullaitivu, Kilinochchi,
  Vavuniya, Mannar and Puttalam districts).
- The **Qibla compass** uses your device's magnetic and motion sensors together
  with the fixed published coordinates of your selected zone. It does not read
  your actual position.

### Information stored only on your device

Your settings are saved in your device's local app storage and a local database:

- Selected prayer zone and data source
- Iqamah offsets and prayer-time adjustments
- Notification preferences and chosen adhan sound
- Display preferences (theme, language, clock style, widget options)
- A cached copy of prayer times and Hijri calendar data, so the app works
  offline

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

---

## Information That Leaves Your Device

Success Call is **not** a fully offline app. Three things involve a network.

### 1. Prayer-time and calendar data

The app downloads prayer times and Hijri calendar data from our static hosting
on GitHub Pages (`successcall.github.io`).

These are plain file downloads — the app sends no personal data. As with any
website, the hosting provider (GitHub) automatically processes your **IP
address** in its standard server logs to deliver the file. We do not receive,
store or have access to those logs.

### 2. Connectivity check

Before deciding how to deliver a prayer notification, the app makes a lightweight
request to `one.one.one.one` (a public Cloudflare endpoint) purely to test
whether an internet connection exists. No data about you is sent; Cloudflare
processes your IP address as it would for any request.

### 3. Prayer notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

*Android and iOS only.*

To deliver prayer-time notifications reliably, the app uses Google Firebase
Cloud Messaging. This involves:

- A **device registration token** generated by Google, which identifies your app
  installation so a notification can reach it. It is not linked to your identity.
- A **topic subscription** matching your selected prayer zone (for example
  `newZones_Zone4`), so your device receives alerts for the right zone. This
  tells our messaging provider which zone you chose, but nothing about who you
  are.

The token is deleted when you uninstall the app. Firebase's handling of this
data is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising or any other
tracking service.**

## Permissions by Platform

Only the permissions relevant to your platform are ever requested, and features
that a platform cannot support are hidden rather than shown as broken options.

### Android

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Prayer time reminders |
| Internet / Network state | Download prayer data, receive notifications |
| Exact alarms | Fire the adhan at the precise prayer minute |
| Run at startup | Restore scheduled prayer alerts after a reboot |
| Ignore battery optimisation *(optional)* | Stop the system delaying prayer alerts |
| Do Not Disturb access *(optional)* | The Silent Mode feature, which mutes your phone during prayer |
| Modify audio settings *(optional)* | Silent Mode |
| Wake lock / full-screen intent | Keep the prayer display awake, show the adhan alert |
| Vibrate | Notification vibration |

The optional permissions are only requested if you turn on the feature that
needs them, and the app works without them.

### iOS

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Prayer time reminders |
| Motion & compass sensors | Qibla direction |

iOS does **not** ask for location, and the app cannot change your ringer, Do Not
Disturb or Focus mode — Apple provides no such capability to apps, so the Silent
Mode feature does not exist in the iOS version.

### Web

The web version stores preferences in your browser's local storage. It does not
send notifications and does not use cookies or trackers.

### Windows

The Windows version stores preferences locally and shows notifications through
the Windows notification system. It does not use Firebase.

## Sensors

The Qibla compass reads your device's **magnetometer and accelerometer** to work
out which way the device is pointing. Sensor readings are used momentarily on
your device to draw the compass and are never recorded or transmitted.

---

## Children's Privacy

Success Call is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal
information from anyone, including children under 13, and there are no accounts,
messaging features or user-generated content.

---

## Data Security and Retention

- Preferences and cached prayer data are stored in your device's app-private
  storage, protected by your operating system.
- We operate no user database, so there is no store of personal data to breach.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data and invalidates the
  notification token.

---

## Your Choices

- **Turn off notifications** in the app or in your system settings at any time.
  This also stops zone topic subscriptions.
- **Use the app offline.** Once prayer times are cached, the app continues to
  work and to show alerts without a network connection.
- **Delete everything** by uninstalling the app, or by using *Reset App* in
  Settings.
